## Hi, I'm Fabio Campolim

Physicist and senior systems analyst in São Paulo, Brazil. Trained at the Institute of
Physics, University of São Paulo (IFUSP) — BSc in Physics and MSc in experimental quantum
and non-linear optics — and since 2025 a PhD candidate at UNICAMP, working on computational
condensed matter. In parallel: 25 years of software engineering, from CDMA systems at
Ericsson's R&D centre, through smart-card and POS firmware and the ERP platforms behind
Brazil's largest e-commerce stores, to my current role at Netcracker Technology supporting
rating & billing systems for global telecom operators. Along the way I spent two years as
a university lecturer, teaching physics, algorithms and mathematics to engineering students.

### 🤖 How I work

My research and much of my professional tooling runs through an AI-assisted workflow
built around Claude Code:

- **Physics on a grid** — finite-difference methods for the Schrödinger, Pauli and Dirac
  equations; band structures, edge states, DOS/LDOS and topological invariants, with every
  derivation checked symbolically before it becomes code
- **Literature at scale** — automated scans across arXiv, OpenAlex, ADS and Scopus, plus
  full reproduction of published results in executable notebooks
- **AI meets the lab bench** — connecting language models to physical instruments,
  starting with a phone as a remote-controlled sensor laboratory
- **AI in production** — built AI-assisted legacy-code analysis and task automation for
  enterprise telecom systems, from scratch, before it was infrastructure

📌 [`claude-session-publisher`](https://github.com/fabiocampolim-design/claude-session-publisher) —
turn Claude Code sessions into self-contained HTML/LaTeX/PDF documents, with a fidelity
report proving nothing was lost along the way.
